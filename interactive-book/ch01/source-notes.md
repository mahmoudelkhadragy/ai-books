=== L4 قانونا كيرشوف (printed 72..) ===
p72: Complex circuits hard by Ohm's law -> German scientist Kirchhoff two laws. First law: current in metals = flow of electrons; by conservation of charge, charge entering a point in a time = charge leaving (Q1=Q2+Q3 branching pipe figure). Statement: sum of currents entering a junction in a closed circuit = sum leaving; OR algebraic sum of currents at a point = 0.
p73: ΣI_in = ΣI_out: I1+I2 = I3+I4; ΣI=0 with in positive, out negative: I1+I2-I3-I4=0. Ex1 junction c: in 4,5,2, out 8, I assumed in: 4+5+2+I=8 -> I=-3A -> 3A outward. Ex2 circuit find I1, I2, I3 (a: 5A to b, b: 4A to c...).
p74: Ex2 solution: b: 5=I1+4 -> I1=1A; c: 4+4=I3 -> 8A; a: I3=5+I2 -> I2=3A. Test15: junction network 2A, I, 4A, 5A, 2A (options 1,2,3,5). Second law: by conservation of energy, electric energy from source in closed path = energy consumed in components. emf = work per unit charge around path once; V = work per unit charge between two points. Statement: algebraic sum of emfs in closed loop = algebraic sum of potential differences across its components (resistances); OR algebraic sum of p.d. in closed loop = 0.
p75: ΣVB = ΣIR: VB1+VB2 = IR1+IR2; ΣV=0: VB1+VB2-IR1-IR2=0. Sign rule (ΣVB=ΣIR): path from - to + inside battery -> +VB; path from + to - inside -> -VB; path same direction as current in R -> +IR; opposite -> -IR.
p76: Sign rule (ΣV=0): battery - to + -> +VB; + to - -> -VB; resistor along current -> -IR; against -> +IR. Ex: a-x-b, b-z-c (4V), a-k-d (2V), b-y-d diagonal, d-c wire: loop adcba: -Vx - Vk + Vz = 0 -> Vx=2V; loop bcdb: Vy - Vz = 0 -> Vy = 4V.
p77: How to solve Kirchhoff problems. Circuit: 12V (a-f), branch b-e: 4||4 + 2 = 4Ω, branch c-d: 2Ω + 5V, f-e 3Ω. Steps: (1) reduce series/parallel groups first; (2) assume unknown currents I1,I2,I3 with directions (not necessarily correct), count unknowns, assume loop directions (CW/CCW); (3) KCL at junction e: I1 = I2 + I3 -> I1 - I2 - I3 = 0 (eq1).
p78: (4) KVL loop abefa: 12 = 4I1 + 3I3 (eq2); (5) repeat until #eqs = #unknowns: loop abcdefa: 12 - 5 = 3I3 - 2I2 (eq3); (6) solve: I1=1.5A, I2=-0.5A, I3=2A; (7) positive -> assumed direction correct; negative -> opposite.
